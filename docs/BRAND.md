# CRUMB brand system

## Core identity

**Name:** CRUMB. **Ticker concept:** $CRUMB. **Tagline:** Small bird. Big crumb energy.

A street-smart, slightly unimpressed pigeon with an oversized crumb, mismatched sneakers, a green neck stripe, and highly expressive eyes. He is proud of a very small achievement and spends too much time online. The voice is dry, playful, and self-aware, never corporate and never a promise of financial returns.

## Palette

| Token | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cream | `#f4f1e9` | Main canvas, text on charcoal |
| Charcoal | `#20221f` | Headlines, outlines, primary actions |
| Acid lime | `#d6f56a` | Hero tape, identity, active states |
| Warm orange | `#f19b72` | Lore and emotional contrast |
| Muted sage | `#a6b394` | Mascot and supporting panels |

The palette avoids gradients. Texture comes from fine dot fields, orbit lines, organic vector shapes, and editorial composition. Typography uses locally available Arial/Helvetica-style sans-serif faces, heavy display settings, light supporting text, and monospaced labels. No font files are bundled or required.

## Mascot system

The full-body character is a layered SVG: head, eyes, pupils, eyelids, body, wing, crumb, shoes, shadow, and mood-specific accessories. The silhouette works as both an expressive hero and a compact icon.

Nine gallery states: original, fancy bird, last crumb, do not disturb, offline bird, terminally online, unbothered, mildly concerned, and space cadet. A happy reaction is used elsewhere in the page. Distinct treatments come from the same source artwork, not unrelated illustrations.

The website can export actual self-contained SVG avatars and meme posters. Editable static brand assets are also in `public/brand/`. Logo SVGs use local font names for editable text; keep the supplied silhouette and spacing when adapting them. Do not invent verified accounts, holder statistics, contract addresses, or a live launch story to fill the design.

## Motion rules

Follow the character rather than adding background spectacle. Eye tracking and the small head rotation acknowledge the pointer. Clicks create a short crumb reaction. Scroll parallax is limited and reveal animations are finite. Repeated motion pauses offscreen and when the visitor selects reduced motion or the pause control. Touch layouts keep the visual identity without depending on hover.
